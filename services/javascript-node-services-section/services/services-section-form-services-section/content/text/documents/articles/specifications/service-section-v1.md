# Service Section v1: sourced sections offering governed Hoare programs

This is the Engineering specification for `org.561.service-section.v1` and
its executable reader in `src/service-section.mjs`. Its field profile is
`SERVICE_SECTION_PROFILE`; its predicates are `SERVICE_SECTION_VOCABULARY`.
The existing `ServicesSectionForm` remains the observation of a section's
directory, package and source custody under the Group's law. A directory
observation and a Service Section reading describe different properties of
the same source owner.

The specification realizes the Group's current pairing: **Rendanheyi is the
outward hull joining a provider to user value; Huidu Guanli is the internal
kernel governing deliberate, revisable settings.** The offered and controlling
programs expose their Hoare obligations. Original SignCells carry these
declarations and their evidence; SignCraft supplies their section reading.

## 1. Exact implemented mathematical carrier

Let `D` be the finite, admitted native SignCell body supplied by the source
owner, and `t` an actually represented token. SignCraft's
`KnowledgeBase.fromCells(D)` retains each original for/against witness in the
original SignChain log. Its `queryToldClaims({token:t})` delegates to
SignCell's token frame and SignGraph's inherited-telling construction.

The frame is the specialization preorder generated by positively witnessed
`(special, "specializes", general)` cells. Its opens are **downsets**, forming
the existing Alexandrov Heyting frame. The telling at a general token
restricts to a specialization. At `t`, SignGraph joins the tellings of the
generalizations `↑t`, reanchoring their subject to `t` and retaining their
source tokens and both witness sides:

\[
  S_D(t)=\bigvee_{g\in\uparrow t}
    \operatorname{restrict}_{g\to t}(\operatorname{Told}_D(g)).
\]

This formula describes the existing implementation; it introduces no new
restriction, equality, evidence-join, or gluing algorithm in this package.
The returned coordinates are the source-represented `(t,predicate,object)`
claims, with their native joined evidence. Each coordinate retains its own
reading and witnesses. Opposing tellings form a contested reading; an absent
claim is not generated as a negative one.

A Service Section reading is this actual source-told section, together with
the offered-program and kernel facets selected from it and from linked
subjects in the **same admitted source cut**. The specification thereby has
an implemented base, source coordinates, restriction owner and evidence
carrier. A package name, a folder listing or a list of HTTP routes supplies
none of those on its own.

Three distinctions are part of the contract:

1. `section.context` is a **declared business/program context**. It does not
   filter `D`, authorize a reader, or establish a transport into another
   context. The supplied cell cut and native token frame are the implemented
   mathematical base of this version.
2. The performed-source kinematic construction in SignGraph's `mark-gluing`
   has its own source-qualified fibers and genuine **upper-open** regions.
   Metrologic's `finiteCoherentSectionSpace` counts compatible choices in that
   specific carrier. This reader does not replace that construction with a
   downset frame, nor claim a chosen kinematic section from its count.
3. Source inheritance does not prove arbitrary sheaf descent, uniqueness,
   program correctness, effect independence, or `H¹ = 0`. Such assertions
   require their own precise base, coefficient/transport data and evidence.

The native snapshot binds both the admitted cell-set address and the
specialization predicate. The reader retains that snapshot unchanged and
reads all pages. It neither creates another snapshot codec nor silently
truncates the section after 128 claims.

## 2. One source document

The source document is plain data with exactly these fields:

```json
{
  "kind": "org.561.service-section.v1",
  "subject": "an original section token",
  "source": {
    "resource": "the originating source resource",
    "revision": "its exact source revision"
  },
  "cells": []
}
```

`subject` may be any Mark admitted by the original cell owner; it need not be
a string. `cells` contains the original native cell presentations, including
their witnesses. Construct them with `@red-cup-engineering/signcraft/cell` or
receive them from an existing qualified owner. Do not author a second local
triple codec or infer positive truth from a bare label. An asserted cell
value unsupported by its witnesses is refused by SignCraft.

The producer supplies an appropriately selected source cut and an immutable
source reference. `source.resource` and `source.revision` are retained
**attribution**, not authentication performed by this pure reader. They do
not overwrite the individual cell witnesses. The reader does not fetch a
revision, grant source access, infer an observation timestamp, or confer an
office. Original host/source custody must establish those relationships.

The reader rejects undeclared envelope fields, accessors and inherited
envelope fields before reading them. It owns the admitted immutable cell
snapshot; later caller mutations cannot change the returned description.
No filesystem access, network request, clock, randomness, callback or service
invocation occurs in `readServiceSection`.

## 3. Normative declaration profile

The new service predicates use `https://561.group/ontology/service-section#`.
The existing Science-owned custody vocabulary retains its identifiers:
`service-section-custody#ServiceSection` and
`service-section-custody#sourceCustody`. The Section type assertion uses
`rdf:type`; specialization remains the original token-frame predicate
`specializes`. The new predicate names are authored by this specification,
not represented as a recovered universal ontology or a new mathematical law.

Every field is plural. The reader preserves alternatives, denials and
contests; it never chooses the first positively told value. A required
declaration is **represented** when its original claim is present, even if
its reading is unknown, denied or contested. Its evidential status remains
visible in the original query entry.

| Facet | Required predicates | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Section | `rdf:type` with the original `ServiceSection` class; `purpose`, `context`, `sourceCustody`, `offers`, `kernel` | The source owner's purpose and declared context, its services, and its internal controls. |
| Offer | `provider`, `user`, `outcome`, `valueMeasure`, `valueAllocation`, `program` | The Rendanheyi commitment joining a provider to an identified user or explicitly parameterized user role, the intended received outcome, its valuation and allocation, and the offered program. |
| Program | `precondition`, `move`, `postcondition`, `preservationObligation` | The actual program's admitted input predicate, transition, promised output predicate and preservation obligation. |
| Kernel | `purpose`, `invariant`, `program`, `governs`, `calibration` | The internal control purpose, binding invariants, control program, programs it governs, and calibrated dimensions. |
| Calibration | `axis`, `admissibleRegion`, `setting`, `revisit` | What is adjustable, its allowed region, the selected setting and the condition/procedure for reconsidering it. |

Every facet also permits `evidence`. Evidence is optional for the structural
read because an unmeasured program must remain representable. Its absence
cannot be converted into successful preservation, successful execution, or
received value. Other predicates remain in the complete native section even
when they are not selected by this profile.

`offers`, `program`, `kernel` and `calibration` point to represented subjects
in the same supplied cut. A reference to an unrepresented subject gets the
original foreign-token refusal. The source owner may supply the missing
source; the reader will not invent an empty foreign section or fetch one.

### Rendanheyi hull

The hull describes what a user can receive and how received value matters.
`outcome` is a user-facing postcondition or outcome reference;
`valueMeasure` identifies the measurement/acceptance basis;
`valueAllocation` identifies how the value is attributed and shared. Price,
payment rail, SKU, HTTP route, MCP tool or A2A skill may be additional claims.
None substitutes for the user, outcome or value relationship.

A reusable source package may explicitly parameterize the user role. An
actual sale or internal engagement must bind the concrete receiver through
the existing runtime/instance owner. Describing a role does not manufacture
a customer or a receipt.

When a chain group is represented, its component services must point to the
same end-user outcome and value-allocation basis. An intermediate schema
match alone does not establish delivery of that outcome. The runtime owner
must qualify each required handoff and preserve the coalition's original
commitment instead of substituting local throughput for user value.

### Hoare programs and evidence

The formal application carrier already has the structure

\[
  (P,C,Q,\operatorname{preserves}),\qquad
  \operatorname{preserves}:\forall x,\;P(x)\to Q(C(x)).
\]

The `preservationObligation` names that obligation for the **actual**
precondition, move and postcondition. A theorem citation must bind the
actual carrier and its hypotheses before it can discharge the obligation.
The generic theorem's existence does not prove an arbitrary service. A
finite behavioral receipt remains evidence about the inputs it exercised.
`evidence` may retain a formal witness, measured receipt, refutation or an
original runtime standing, with its exact source and identity.

The general runtime owner is CSE's existing `deriveProgramContract` /
`readProgramContractStanding`, reached through operation declaration and
surface induction. A consuming adapter should derive these program fields
from that original contract and use its exact contract identity. It should
carry the original matching receipts without reconstructing its own
measurement fold. Explicitly unmeasured ingestion uses an explicit empty
receipt array; the CSE default performs its own bounded measurement.
The current CSE boundary binds the schema content and its explicit reference
graph through the original descriptor. A repeated schema name alone cannot
attach an older predicate's receipt. Unexposed ambient evaluator state and
host callback implementation remain separate source/execution obligations.

The x402 service contract is one payment/runtime specialization. Its offer,
MCP, A2A and ingress projections remain derived from that owner. The general
Service Section specification does not require a payment rail and does not
become a second x402 contract or operation registry.

Composition additionally needs the actual intermediate-predicate/transport
obligation (for example, `Q₁ ⇒ P₂` under the specified handoff), its evidence,
and preserved effects/invariants. A matching schema address is useful
incidence data; this reader does not promote it to a composition proof.

### Huidu kernel

The kernel's `program` is its **control transition**. Its `governs` claims
name the offered program subjects that the kernel is meant to govern. The
two programs may differ. The reader returns a `governance` entry for every
offered program, preserving every matching incoming `governs` entry. Empty
`bindings` explicitly exposes a missing governance relationship.

A calibration is a chosen point in a declared admissible region on a named
axis, with a stated revisit condition. The admissible region may be a typed
finite choice, an order interval or another original domain carrier; this
reader does not invent a scalar scale for every decision. The revision
condition may cite changed evidence, a boundary crossing or a scheduled
control operation. The pure reader does not consult a clock or run it.

Invariants remain binding. Grey-scale calibration applies to the dimensions
the institution leaves adjustable; it does not mean partially enforcing a
law, silently accepting a failed postcondition, or discarding opposition.
The control program's own Hoare obligation must explain how allowed changes
preserve its governing invariants and the offered commitments. This version
makes that declaration/evidence inspectable; the installed execution owner
must receive and enforce the control transition.

The existing enterprise owner already implements the chosen-setting
transition. `enterprise-setting-record` checks the enterprise's declared
axis and judgment, verifies its original doer authority, and writes through
the existing enterprise setting store. `resourceLanePolicyFromSetting`
projects an actual judged percentage setting into a resource-lane policy
at an explicit `evaluatedAt`; it refuses a default, expired judgment or
unqualified source record. Its own output distinguishes that projection
from host selection and work authorization.

An enterprise adapter must reuse that source: `axis` is the original axis,
`admissibleRegion` its poles/domain, `setting` the recorded value, `revisit`
the original `revisitBy`, and `evidence` the original attributed judgment
and any actual policy projection. The kernel's control program is the
original setting operation; `governs` names only programs that actually
consume the setting. Use the original judgment identity/revision, not the
axis name alone, for a calibration subject. Distinct historical judgments
are not automatically contradictory current settings. Retain the full
history while using the original owner to select at an explicit time.
Growing evidence does not require a calibration to move only upward.

## 4. Reader and normalization result

```js
import { readServiceSection } from
  "@red-cup-engineering/agentic-organization-services-section-engineering-services-section/service-section";

const reading = readServiceSection(sourceDocument);
```

The result retains:

- `document`: the immutable admitted input, with native canonical cells;
- `snapshot` and `sourceCells`: the original interpretation-bound snapshot
  and admitted cell-set identity;
- `sourceSection`: all original query pages and their entries;
- `section`, `hull.offers`, `kernels`: fields referencing those exact entries
  and the linked subjects' original readings;
- `governance`: the declared kernel bindings for each offered program;
- `missing`: required declarations absent from the source cut;
- `contests`: native contested claim entries in the read facets.

`contests` does not claim to enumerate every possible logical inconsistency
or every contested specialization path. The complete original input cells
remain in `document.cells`, including specialization evidence. Different
positively told settings also remain distinct values; the reader does not
pretend that functional uniqueness has been proved.

There is no scalar approval, execution status, sale permission or universal
conformance flag. `missing: []` means only that the required declarations
are represented. Empty governance bindings, unknown/contested claims and
absent evidence remain independent questions.

The `read-service-section` command uses the existing JavaScript Node
operation host to read one JSON document per input line and return the same
reading. The filesystem-facing `readServicesSectionTree` discovers
`data/records/service-section.json` alongside the existing form observation.
Its section entry includes `serviceSection`, or `null` when that document
is absent. A present malformed document is refused, not reported as absent.
No package program is imported or run merely because its section is read.

The existing `foundServicesSection` operation accepts that same optional
source document. It validates supplied and existing documents before
founding writes, creates a new record through the original File owner's
exclusive-create operation, and preserves an existing or concurrent
record. Its `serviceSection` result is read from the persisted bytes, so a
requested new document cannot be reported as installed when an older
source was retained. Omission keeps the existing directory-only behavior.

This supplies a concrete migration: existing sections add their source-owned
cell document; the current tree reader exposes the normalized service
facets without rewriting historical claims or treating a directory name as
a mathematical or commercial certificate. L-Space consumes the same cells
through the same SignCraft readers, rather than a second knowledge index.

## 5. Producing sections through the same profile

`extendServiceSection(document, declarations)` is the pure producer paired
with the reader. It takes a source document and explicit role declarations:

```js
const reading = extendServiceSection(document, [{
  role: "program",
  subject: originalContract.id,
  fields: {
    precondition: [originalContract.precondition],
    move: [originalContract.move],
    postcondition: [originalContract.postcondition],
    preservationObligation: [originalContract],
    evidence: originalReceipts,
  },
  witness: originalDerivationWitness,
}]);
```

The role and field names come from `SERVICE_SECTION_PROFILE`. Each field
is plural. There is no default customer, allocation, kernel, successful
receipt or preservation witness. An empty field contributes no claim.
Each produced cell is a telling by the explicit supplied witness. A
telling whose object is a denied receipt retains that receipt as denied;
the existence of attributed data is not the success of its embedded claim.

The original SignCraft `retainedCarrier`, `cell` and `cellSet` own data
admission, immutable retention and merge. The producer interprets the exact
admitted data fields; the upstream carrier treats an object field with
`undefined` value as absent. Indexed and named accessors are refused before
iteration. Existing source support and opposition remain alongside the
new telling; reapplying the identical witnessed declaration is idempotent.
The returned reading and `reading.document` are consumed by the existing
reader and L-Space without an alternative graph representation.

Downstream program producers share an input envelope
`{document, bindings}`. `admitServiceSectionProjection` admits that data
before the program owner interprets bindings. It does not resolve linked
programs prematurely: the producer supplies their native claims before the
complete section is read. Binding semantics remain with the original
operation or service-contract owner.

CSE's operation projection derives technical fields from its actual
operation contracts and matches the original behavioral receipts. Explicit
`{operation, offer}` bindings supply section/offer/program incidence. An
unadvertised control operation may still supply a program in the same cut;
it does not become an offer just because it is present. Induced discovery
and its existing discovery resource consume this reading. A host-boundary
declaration is not an immutable identity of an arbitrary callback's
implementation; exact source loading and execution evidence remain with
the host.

The x402 projection uses admitted Hoare service contracts and explicit
`{contract, offer}` bindings. Its program subject retains the original
contract ID, version and complete contract digest; changing contract bytes
cannot alias the same program merely by retaining a route/version label.
Its precondition, service binding, postcondition and refusal obligations
come from that contract. Market derivation, ingress catalog and the market
CLI consume the same reading. CLI output includes a native
`service-section.json` that the existing tree reader can receive. The
projection does not enable a held sale, invoke compute, verify or settle
payment, or replace package/source receiving with contract authorship.

These consumers are downstream of the common pure form package. The form
reader does not import CSE, x402, enterprise storage or a runtime host. A
consumer's exact package publication, native lock preparation and frozen
installation remain separate from source integration and local composition.

## 6. Scientific control, transported invariants and resource receiving

A Service Section realizes its user-facing commitment through a resource-bounded Hoare program and a Huidu kernel that controls that program. The kernel MUST distinguish the quantity it intends to improve from the invariants its permitted moves preserve. Its science becomes operational through explicit carriers, source-qualified measurements, bounded plans, execution receipts and received outcomes. These declarations use the existing profile and native witnessed cells; producers add them through `extendServiceSection`, and readers retain their evidence and opposition through `readServiceSection`.

### 6.1 Mathematical carriers and actual controls

The gyrobifastigium is the existing J26 carrier. Its scientific construction derives an oriented exterior from two triangular prisms joined along a quarter-turned square. `J26.faceVertices`, `every_face_boundary_is_valid` and `face_dim_drop` supply concrete incidence and dimension obligations [S1]. Metrologic supplies that geometry; SignGraph's `j26CellularSurface` and `j26FacePatch` derive finite cellular surfaces and explicitly glued patches. A producer using this model MUST identify its exact interfaces, including orientation-reversing bijections of matching faces. The section's user/provider relation remains an independently declared service relation.

SignGraph already supplies two different transport diagnostics. `burgersOfComplex` computes exact translational residues on admitted oriented complexes. `affineHolonomyOfComplex` transports rotations and translations together through `sixfoldCompose`. For motions `(k,t)` and `(l,u)`, the ordered product is `(k+l, t+R^k u)`. A reversed traversal uses the group inverse. “Clockwise” MUST therefore be qualified by the selected orientation and frame: reversing a word, negating translations and changing reference frames are distinct operations. Face residues, global cycle residues and conservation balances remain separately inspectable.

Where its contract declares a protected residual, the kernel MUST preserve that residual while pursuing the specified reparable objective. The existing theorem `unpaired_disclination_survives_descent` gives an operationally useful example: every slide stage preserves the unpaired five-site's rotational frame, while the dipole configuration's translation collapses at its maximum separation [S2]. Adding an opposite cone is a different transformation. A successful program can consequently finish with a nonzero preserved residual. There is no universal “all defects zero” normal form. The source owner determines which mathematical invariants represent protected obligations and which admissible operations may alter them.

The Dervish bridge is also precise: `five_site_link_is_kcbs_pentagon` separates a thirty-step boundary into mod-five link and mod-six frame coordinates; `link_vertex_never_redevelops` preserves the distinction between repeated visits and developed states [S3]. Consumers MUST retain that state when using this carrier. The golden-field identification of the two charges remains an explicit research obligation [S4]. The proposed sequencing–whirling limit bridge likewise remains open [S5]. The existing vortex theorem supplies a narrower usable implication: a gradient has zero circulation, so nonzero circulation refutes gradient exactness on the specified loop [S9]. Zero circulation on one tested loop alone does not construct a global potential. Existing finite diagnostics can be used immediately without promoting the proposed bridges into control theorems.

### 6.2 Attention, resource allocation and user value

Attention allocation has an existing positive contract: selected reconciliation costs fit a budget; adding a selected obstruction cannot decrease accumulated cost; feasibility is downward closed; and greater captured benefit leaves no greater residual [S6]. The kernel MUST name the candidate obligations, cost grade, benefit measure and budget before selecting work. Predicted benefit and subsequently received improvement are separate witnessed claims about the same obligation.

The exact attention solver establishes optimal feasible selections while exhibiting equal-valued selections that depend on fold direction [S7]. The chosen tie rule therefore belongs in calibration. The service MUST expose its objective, deterministic tie policy and solver exactness. Consumers needing shared bottleneck allocation use the original SignCraft `composeCellResourceFlow`, `assessSharedTransportPressure` and `planSharedTransportAllocation` route, whose upstream resource solver accounts for shared source identities. Maximum benefit, minimum expenditure and max-min fairness are distinct objectives; each offered program declares the one it realizes.

Resource evidence MUST distinguish observation, estimate and hypothetical model, retaining unit, source revision, observation time, system boundary and applicable uncertainty. Physical EROEI additionally identifies delivered and invested energy, conversion stage, service mix, accounting period, excluded costs and a positive investment denominator [S8]. Native quantities such as inference tokens and execution time remain useful resource grades in their own right. A conversion into joules requires an identified meter or a separately evidenced conversion model.

### 6.3 Requirements and receiving cases

For a governed move with state `x` and supplied calibration `theta`, the intended obligation has the form

\[
 P(x,\theta)\land I(x)\land A(x,\theta)
 \;\Longrightarrow\; Q(C_\theta(x))\land I(C_\theta(x)),
\]

where `A` is the declared admissible-region/resource condition and `I` is the declared preserved invariant. The control program that changes `theta` has its own obligation to retain admissibility and the governed commitments. This is a specification of what needs evidence; it is not a new proof rule or a guarantee of optimality. A mathematical invariant becomes an institutional obligation only through the actual source owner's declaration.

The following obligations refine the current declaration profile. Scientific carrier requirements apply when a program uses or promises those carrier-specific properties; J26 geometry is not required of every service. They do not add a parallel schema. The acceptance cases belong with the original producer or consuming assay owner, while the form reader verifies faithful projection and source retention.

### 6.5 A strict source-declaration path category and its limits

The [Science categorical-service research draft](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/pull/360) separates three things the v1 reader previously left implicit: **a strict category of typed declaration paths**, a proposed context-indexed semantic program category, and a mathematical sheaf/descent claim. Only the first is implemented in this package. Do not claim that a declaration path is an executable program, a Lean-preserved Hoare theorem, an acceptable sale, or a mathematical global section.

The exports `identityServiceDeclarationPath`, `offerServiceDeclarationPath`, and `composeServiceDeclarationPaths` use the **existing v1 reading and original SignCraft native Mark coincidence** rather than another graph or proof codec. One generator requires a selected offer/program link actually represented and positively witnessed in `readServiceSection`, with its four Hoare declaration fields present. Its domain and codomain are **explicit interface Marks supplied by the original typed-operation owner**, never inferred from English pre/postcondition strings. Original source, snapshot, offer/program subject, link cell and support/opposition stay with the step. A contested positive link may remain represented as a *syntactic* generator; its opposition is preserved, never promoted into authority or verified correctness.

A path has typed domain/codomain, a sequence of selected steps, and **standing: syntactic-only**. The empty path is its identity. Composition is step concatenation and refuses mismatched intermediate native interface Marks, including any **resource residual, rights, receiver or revision identity encoded in them**. Identity and associativity are therefore strict under the same stable native Mark coincidence. The API cannot derive or verify that the stated residual resource state is truthful; the actual resource/contract owner still owes that judgment. No function produces approval, proof, execution, invoice, payment or accepted customer benefit.

| Gate | Current source-qualified behavior | Still due from original owner |
| --- | --- | --- |

| SS-CAT-05 | The v1 offer still describes user, value measure and value allocation separately from the program. | Receiving enterprise and economic ledgers must establish terminal customer acceptance, external consideration, rights, supplier allocation and net economic effect, not merely an x402 route or internal credit. |
| SS-CAT-06 | The original evidence cut and token specialization frame remain source-reading facts; the new path type is a separate derived syntactic carrier. | Science must supply context restrictions, identity/composition laws on reindexing, any Grothendieck indexed model, sheaf covers/gluing countermodels and formal semantics before a global-section claim. |

It **does not** test host authorization or prove the customer's Hoare postcondition. The prior twelve scientific-control obligations (SS-SCI-01…12) remain independent and unchanged.

## 7. Receiving boundaries and acceptance

The executable acceptance cases cover actual inherited support plus local
opposition; missing versus explicit unknown claims; native pagination;
immutable source retention; linked-subject admission; the full hull/program/
kernel profile; distinct control and offered programs with explicit
governance; CLI parity; and the existing forest consumer. The hypothetical
fixture is labeled as such and supplies no company sale or execution claim.

Source qualification, a published package, an installed host, a live offer,
an invoked program and received user value are distinct observations. The
present source specification and tests establish the first of those for
their exercised scope. Runtime adoption must retain the exact document,
source snapshot, program identities, kernel bindings and actual receipts.
